Netlify form setup notes

What was fixed:
- Quote form posts with method="POST" and data-netlify="true".
- Hidden form-name value is quote-request.
- Form action now goes to /thank-you/ so Netlify records the submission first, then shows thank-you.html.
- Redirect rules include both /thank-you and /thank-you/.

After deploying:
1. Open Netlify > Site > Forms and confirm quote-request appears after a fresh deploy.
2. Submit one test quote from the live Netlify/custom domain, not from your local computer.
3. To email the client: Netlify > Site configuration > Forms > Form notifications > Add notification > Email notification.
4. Choose form quote-request and enter the client email address.
